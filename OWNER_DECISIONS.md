# Decisions before report automation

The blank observation worksheet can organize records now. It is not an approved professional inspection method. These decisions remain unresolved.

| Decision | Responsible reviewer | Evidence needed | Behavior until resolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Report purpose and governing standard | Responsible professional and requesting party | Written scope; applicable standard/form and edition | Generic observation record only |
| Qualified roles and allowed methods | Responsible professional | Verified relevant qualifications and method/safety scope | No prefilled inspector, credential or testing instructions |
| Required observations, units and access limitations | Responsible professional | Defined methods and handling of inaccessible/untested components | Record status and evidence without a condition score |
| Interpretations and escalation wording | Responsible professional | Criteria supporting each conclusion and follow-up | No automatic verdict or compliance statement |
| Business service, fee and delivery commitments | Business owner | Approved current service terms | No price or turnaround promise |
| Sign-off and revisions | Business owner / responsible professional | Authorized reviewer, approval and correction process | Blank approval; draft status until reviewed |
| Client records and distribution | Business owner | Storage, access, retention, recipient and consent policy | Completed records remain private; no export/email automation |

A future implementation must preserve unknown/not-accessed/not-tested values and provenance across input and output. It must never turn a missing observation into a favorable result. PDF, JSON or workbook claims require an actual implementation and demonstrated output review.
